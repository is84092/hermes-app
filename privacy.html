Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

Hermes Personal Assistant is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • Data accessed: the owner's own Gmail messages, solely to send and read email at the owner's request.
  • Storage: OAuth tokens and any data stay on the owner's own computer. Nothing is uploaded to third-party servers by this app.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone.
  • Use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revocation: access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

隱私權政策

本工具僅供擁有者本人使用，只在擁有者要求時存取其本人的 Gmail 以寄送與讀取郵件。授權憑證與資料只保存在擁有者自己的電腦上，不會出售、分享或揭露給任何人。可隨時於 https://myaccount.google.com/permissions 撤銷授權。
